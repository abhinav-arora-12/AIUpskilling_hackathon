# Access Control and Roles

Three roles exist in the operations platform.

- Operator: read access to all policies and records.
- Supervisor: may approve Tier 2 changes.
- Reviewer: may approve Tier 3 changes. A reviewer cannot approve their own request.
- Access is reviewed every quarter by the compliance team. Related: [[SOP-HITL-001]].

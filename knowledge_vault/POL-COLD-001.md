# Cold Chain Storage

Refrigerated stock is stored between 2 C and 8 C.

- Any temperature excursion must be logged within 15 minutes of detection.
- A product that has two excursions in one month is moved to quarantine.
- Quarantined stock cannot be released until [[POL-LOT-002]] returns GREEN.

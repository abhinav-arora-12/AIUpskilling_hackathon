# SKU Naming Standard

SKU identifiers use the format SKU-### with exactly three zero-padded digits, for example SKU-099.

- Legacy SKUs with two digits must be migrated to the three-digit form by 2027-03-31.
- Product records are listed in [[CAT-PROD-001]].
